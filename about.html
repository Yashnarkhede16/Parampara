Born from Tradition

✦ ✦ ✦

Paramparik Collection was founded by Neha Borole in Asoda, Maharashtra, with a deep love for India's rich jewellery heritage. Every Nath, Bindi, Bridal Choker, Mangalsutra, and Earring is lovingly handcrafted using traditional techniques passed down through generations.

Whether you choose a ready-to-ship festive ornament or request a custom bridal set via WhatsApp, each creation is crafted with patience, precision, and timeless beauty.
