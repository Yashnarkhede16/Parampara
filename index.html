About Paramparik Collection

Made with Tradition, Worn with Pride

Paramparik Collection was founded by Neha Borole at Ram Mandir, Asoda, with a deep love for India's rich jewellery heritage. Every Nath, Bindi, Bridal Choker, Mangalsutra, and Earring is handcrafted with patience, authentic pearls, and traditional Maharashtrian artistry.
